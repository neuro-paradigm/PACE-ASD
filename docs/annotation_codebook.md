# Codebook: visual annotation of the recordings (fourth phase, E7)

Two authors annotate the 95 recordings independently. A third author, who
does not annotate, generates the material and keeps the key.

## Material

Generated by `python scripts/visual_audit_phase4.py --kit` in
`results/phase4/annotation/` (never published or shared: it shows children):

* `sheets/R01.jpg` ... `R95.jpg`: one sheet per recording. Top left: the first
  frame in which a pose was detected, without overlay. Then eight frames evenly
  spaced from the first to the last detected frame, numbered 1-8, with the
  estimated skeleton drawn (green lines; left-side points blue, right-side and
  face points red).
* `settings_contact_sheet.jpg`: the first frame of every recording, labelled
  with its code, for grouping recordings into settings.
* `annotator_A.csv`, `annotator_B.csv`: one row per code to fill in.
* `key_do_not_open_before_consensus.csv`: code to child. Only the author who
  generated the material may open it, and only after the consensus.

Frames are shown at a common height; their widths differ because the videos
were cropped differently. Ignore the width.

## Rules

* Work alone; do not look at the other annotator's file, at the AI-assisted
  annotation (`results/phase3/qc/visual_audit.csv`), at the key or at any
  file that names children.
* Fill every cell with `yes` or `no` (items 1-3) or a label (item 4). If
  unsure, choose the more likely answer and write the reason in `note`.

## Items

1. `walks_toward_camera`: **yes** if, over most of the eight numbered frames,
   the child walks towards the camera (the child grows in the frame and the
   legs alternate). **no** if the child mostly sits, stands, turns away, walks
   across the frame, is carried or led, or runs up to the camera after
   another activity.
2. `other_person_visible`: **yes** if any part of another person (adult or
   child), other than the child being recorded, is visible in any frame.
3. `gross_tracking_error`: **yes** if in at least one numbered frame the
   skeleton is on another person, or most of its points are clearly not on the
   child (for example the whole skeleton displaced or collapsed). A wrong
   wrist or foot alone is **no**.
4. `setting`: a short label for the place (for example `room with sofa`).
   After items 1-3 are done for all recordings, open the contact sheet and give
   recordings that were filmed in the same place exactly the same label. Same
   place means the same room or outdoor area seen from a similar position,
   recognisable from fixed features (walls, doors, floor, furniture).

## Consensus

After both files are complete, the two annotators compare them, discuss every
disagreement while looking at the sheets, and write `consensus.csv` (same
columns). Then run `python scripts/visual_audit_phase4.py --agreement`, which
reports agreement and switches every analysis to the consensus.
